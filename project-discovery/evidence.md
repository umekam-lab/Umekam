# Evidence register

Compiled 22 September 2026. This file lists the claims the recommendation rests on, where each came from, how far it has been checked, the calculations, and what is still missing. The full scan notes, with about 550 source URLs, are in `research_notes/Rune venture discovery Denmark/r1`–`r6`.

## 1. Method and its limits

- **Scope:** six parallel research streams, 20 problem/opportunity pairs, Denmark first, with Sweden, Norway and the UK as comparison or supply sources. Searches were run in Danish and English.
- **Access date:** 2026-09-22 for every source.
- **Verification level: search extract only.** The session's web-search cap (200 calls) ran out during the scan, and the network policy blocked direct page fetches for every domain tried, including retsinformation.dk, dst.dk, at.dk, ski.dk, kfst.dk, mst.dk, supplier sites and Wikipedia. Every "fact" below is what the search engine's summary said the page contains. The page itself was not read. Figures may be paraphrased, and publication dates are often missing. **Before any money is committed, re-open the sources for claims marked ★ (decision-critical).**
- **Labels:**
  - **F** = observed in a source (search extract)
  - **B** = background knowledge, not checked in this session
  - **E** = estimate or calculation
  - **H** = hypothesis
- **Confidentiality:** no founder, employer, family or student information was used in any query.

## 4. Calculations

All calculations are reproducible with `python3 analysis/economics.py` and `python3 analysis/scoring.py`.

**A: contribution per order (distributor).** Contribution = O × d − O × f − h × 450 − 0.02 × O, where O is order value, d the distributor discount, f the freight share and h the installation hours.
- Mid: 45,000 × 0.32 − 45,000 × 0.05 − 3 × 450 − 900 = 14,400 − 2,250 − 1,350 − 900 = **DKK 9,900 (22%)**.
- Low (30k, 25%, 7%, 4 h): **DKK 3,000 (10%)**. High (80k, 38%, 3%, 2 h): **DKK 25,500 (32%)**.
- Break-even: fixed costs ÷ contribution per order. Mid: 60,000 ÷ 9,900 ≈ **6 orders a year**.
- Year 1 (mid): revenue 10 × 45,000 + 10 × 0.4 × 15,000 = **DKK 510,000**; contribution 99,000 + 60,000 × 0.22 = **DKK 112,200**; after fixed costs **DKK 52,200**.
- Working capital: 3 overlapping orders × 45,000 × (0.68 cost of goods − 0.30 customer deposit) ≈ **DKK 51,000**, giving a planning range of DKK 40k–80k.

**B: staff alarm reseller, per site.** 12 devices × DKK 199 × 12 months × 25% = DKK 7,164, plus set-up DKK 10,000 × 60% = DKK 6,000, giving **DKK 13,164 in year 1** (range 6,218–19,757).

**C: care-home unit clearance.** 7,500 − 4,500 subcontractor − 4 h × 250 = **DKK 2,000 per job** (range 0–3,750).

**D: board maintenance planning.** DKK 500,000 of annual recurring revenue ÷ DKK 3,000 a year = **167 associations** (100–333).

**E: holiday-home monitoring.** Monthly contribution 129 − 45 = DKK 84; DKK 500,000 ÷ (84 × 12) = **496 subscribers** (365–947).
