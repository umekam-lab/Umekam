## Pair 1 – Estate clearance (dødsbo-rydning) and senior moves (seniorflytning / flytning til plejebolig)

### Takeaway
This is a real, paid-for problem. Dozens of Danish clearance firms publish prices: about 4,000–7,000 kr ex. VAT to clear a plejebolig, and 75–99 kr/m² ex. VAT for a home. A gig platform shows many completed "move to plejehjem" tasks. Senior-move assistants charge about 560–595 kr/hour. The trigger is sharp and deadline-driven: about 58,000 deaths a year, rent liability, and plejecentre wanting the unit emptied within days. The market is crowded with SEO-heavy van-and-labour firms. The more open space is a trust-based, turnkey coordinator service for relatives who live far away, which suits the founder's strengths better than running a crew.

The reference year was not visible in the snippet and is probably around 2020–2021 — [DST NYT: Fortsat færre ældre bor i pleje- og ældreboliger](https://www.dst.dk/da/Statistik/udgivelser/NytHtml?cid=32187) (undated in snippet).
- [Fact] In 2022, 71,900 people of all ages lived in a pleje- or ældrebolig. **Rules on vacating and rent (purchasing trigger and deadline)**
- [Fact] A pleje- or ældrebolig is terminated with the notice period in the lease, typically 3 months. If the resident dies without a spouse or other household member, notice can be 1 month. Notice must be written and runs to the 1st of a month. The estate pays rent until the unit is re-let or the notice period ends, and the landlord must re-let as quickly as possible. A skifteretsattest is normally required, but many plejebolig landlords accept termination before it is issued — [Ældre Sagen: Fraflytning og opsigelse af pleje- eller ældrebolig](https://www.aeldresagen.dk/viden-og-raadgivning/vaerd-at-vide/b/bolig/aeldreboliger/fraflytning-og-opsigelse-af-pleje-eller-aeldrebolig-samt-plejehjem) (undated).
- [Fact] Albertslund Kommune's internal procedure: when a plejebolig resident dies, the municipality terminates the lease with notice to the 1st of the current month plus one month, and the unit must be cleared before the inspection (syn) — [Albertslund procedure PDF "Dødsfald – tømning af bolig, opsigelse af bolig m.m."](https://medarbejdersiden.albertslund.dk/media/4556057/doedsfald-toemning-af-bolig-opsigelse-af-bolig-mm-d080420.pdf) (undated; file name suggests 2020-04-08).
- [Fact] Plejehjem typically want the unit cleared as fast as possible, "preferably within a few days". Practice varies: some homes have staff pack belongings and store them internally or externally. In several court districts, the skifteret and the municipality have agreements allowing clearance before the estate's administration is decided — search summary drawing on [Antika: Hvornår må man rydde et dødsbo](https://antika.dk/blog/hvornaar-rydde-doedsbo/) (undated) and [Retsinformation VEJ nr. 9396 af 08/08/2012 – Vejledning til kommuner om deponering og rydning af indbo efter afdød plejehjemsbeboer](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/retsinfo/2012/9396) (2012-08-08, listed as "Gældende" in a PDF printout dated 15 July 2026: [PDF](https://www.retsinformation.dk/api/pdf/143091)).

### Inferences
- **User / buyer / budget holder** [Hyp]: The user is the older person moving, or the estate. The buyer is usually an adult child or the estate executor (bobestyrer, or the heirs in a privately administered estate). The budget is the estate's assets, or the senior's own funds for a move. Secondary referrers are plejecentre and kommunal visitation, bedemænd, estate lawyers and banks.
- **Trigger and frequency** [Est]: It is a one-off purchase per family event, and repeat buying by the same customer is rare. Recurring volume therefore depends on referral channels (plejecentre, bedemænd, lawyers), not repeat customers. Plejebolig turnover can be estimated from about 42,000 plejehjem/plejebolig units (36,900 + 4,800 + 700 in the DST split above). Assuming an average stay of 2–3 years (a [Hyp] that needs verification against the Ældre Sagen/VIVE resident fact pages), that is roughly 14,000–21,000 move-ins and a similar number of vacated units per year. Adding about 58,000 deaths a year, of which only some leave a home to clear, gives an addressable event flow in the tens of thousands per year.
- **Rough market size** [Est, low confidence]: Assume 10,000–20,000 paid clearances or senior moves a year at 4,000–12,000 kr each. That gives roughly 40–240 m kr a year of clearance/move labour, excluding resale value. This is a wide band built only from the price points above.
- **Cost of leaving it unresolved** [Est]: Rent for 1–3 months under the notice rules. Monthly plejebolig rent was not found; a 5,000–8,000 kr/month assumption is an unverified [Hyp]. Also travel costs and lost workdays for relatives living far away, and plejecenter pressure because of the "within a few days" clearance expectation. The municipality can deposit or clear belongings under VEJ 9396/2012 (who pays: not found).
- **Why switch / newcomer improvement** [Hyp]: The incumbents are van-and-labour firms whose margin partly comes from buying valuables (opkøb offset). That creates a trust tension for heirs: is the offset fair? A newcomer could sell a turnkey, remote-relative-friendly coordinator service: video walkthrough, photo inventory, sorting of heirlooms among siblings, auction consignment via Lauritz/Campen with transparent pass-through of proceeds, donation to genbrug, documented handover to the plejecenter, and one fixed-price quote with a deadline guarantee. Lenore (560–595 kr/h) shows this coordinator role is sold today, but it is thinly supplied.
- **Why incumbents might not copy** [Hyp]: Clearance firms optimise for truck utilisation and opkøb margin. Transparent pass-through of resale value weakens their model, and pedagogical, relational handling of a grieving or cognitively impaired older person is not their core skill. The counter-argument is that a firm like Nordsjællands Borydning already targets plejehjem specifically, and the coordinator layer itself is easy to copy.
- **Founder fit** [Hyp]: Strong on trust-building, documentation, direct sales, and handling vulnerable people and families (a social-pedagogical background suits relatives of people with dementia). Weak spots: limited weekly time conflicts with deadlines of a few days, and physical labour needs subcontractors or employees. A coordinator model that subcontracts the heavy lifting to 2–3 clearance and moving partners fits better than owning vans.
- **Capital** [Est]: Test (landing page, local Google Ads, flyers and meetings with 5–10 plejecentre and bedemænd, liability insurance): about 10,000–30,000 kr. Coordinator-model launch: about 30,000–80,000 kr. Own crew and van: about 150,000–400,000 kr (van lease or purchase, trailer, insurance, first wages). All figures are unverified estimates.
- **Contribution margin** [Est]: Coordinator hours at about 560 kr/h (Lenore benchmark) plus a margin on subcontracted clearance (4,000–7,000 kr jobs) could plausibly give a 30–50% contribution margin on the coordinator share. Own-crew margins at 350–500 kr/h depend on labour costs. None of this is verified.
- **Biggest red flags**: (1) Heavy SEO competition, with firms running dozens of city pages, so paid acquisition may be expensive. (2) Deadline pressure conflicts with limited weekly time. (3) Low-price gig channel (Handyhand, average about 2,000 kr for a plejehjem move) anchors prices low. (4) Some plejecentre pack belongings themselves, and families use free charity pickups or do it themselves ("do nothing" substitute). (5) Legal care is needed around skifteret rules before removing assets.

## Pair 2 – Moisture, mould and indoor-climate monitoring for Danish rental housing

### Takeaway
Mould in rentals is clearly a live legal and administrative issue. Landlords owe a sound dwelling, tenants can claim rent reduction, compensation or termination, tenants must ventilate, and municipalities run "Sund og sikker bolig" and skimmel functions. BL publishes guidance. But in this session **I found no evidence of sensor spend, tenders, sensor prices or mould-specific 2025 legislation**. The December 2025 almene-housing agreement found here does not mention skimmel in the snippet. Demand evidence is Weak pending a proper follow-up.

### Cited Findings
- [Fact] BL – Danmarks Almene Boliger publishes a knowledge page "Skimmel i boligen" with practical guidance on handling mould cases in almene boliger. Figures not retrieved — [BL: Skimmel i boligen](https://bl.dk/viden-kartotek/skimmel-i-boligen/) (undated).
- [Fact] Several municipalities run dedicated mould and unhealthy-housing pages for tenants, including private rentals: [Aalborg: Sund og sikker bolig](https://www.aalborg.dk/mit-liv/min-bolig/ejerbolig/indeklima-og-varme/sund-og-sikker-bolig/); [Roskilde: Skimmelsvamp i privat udlejningsbolig](https://www.roskilde.dk/da-dk/service-og-selvbetjening/borger/bolig-og-byggeri/byggeri/skimmelsvamp-i-privat-udlejningsbolig/); [Glostrup: Skimmelsvamp i din bolig](https://www.glostrup.dk/borger/bolig-og-byggeri/sund-og-sikker-bolig/skimmelsvamp-i-din-bolig); [Rudersdal](https://rudersdal.dk/borger/bolig-og-byggeri/dit-hus-og-grund/skimmelsvamp-i-boliger-og-opholdsrum); [Rebild](https://rebild.dk/borger/min-bolig/skimmelsvamp) (all undated). This shows a municipal enforcement channel exists; case volumes were not retrieved.
- [Fact] Under Danish rent law the landlord must deliver the dwelling in good and sound condition. Where mould stems from conditions the landlord is responsible for, tenants can claim repair, proportional rent reduction while the defect lasts, compensation for damaged belongings and extra costs, health-related compensation, and in serious cases termination. Tenants must maintain a good indoor climate (ventilation, not drying laundry indoors) — search summary from secondary legal-guidance sites: [Digura: skimmelsvamp som lejer](https://digura.dk/skimmelsvamp-alt-du-skal-vide-som-lejer/); [Dansk Skimmel: information til lejere](https://www.dansk-skimmel.dk/viden-om/information-til-lejere/); [Nævn.dk: skimmelsvamp i lejebolig](https://www.xn--nvn-yla.dk/guide/skimmelsvamp-lejebolig) (all undated). These are secondary sources; the primary law text was not retrieved.
- [Fact] In December 2025 the government, SF, Konservative and Enhedslisten agreed "Aftale om styrkede rammer for alment byggeri". It includes 500 m kr (2025–2029) for almene seniorbofællesskaber, 150 m kr (2026–2031) for temporary rent reductions, and measures on empty homes, including those empty due to "serious building-technical conditions". The snippet showed no mould-specific provision — [Socialministeriet: Ny aftale skal sikre flere almene boliger](https://www.sm.dk/nyheder/nyhedsarkiv/2025/dec/ny-aftale-skal-sikre-flere-almene-boliger) (Dec 2025); [Agreement PDF](https://www.sm.dk/Media/639014943075256840/Aftale%20om%20styrkede%20rammer%20for%20alment%20byggeri.pdf) (Dec 2025).
- [Fact] Landsbyggefonden has opened funding pools for "bæredygtighed og digitalisering" in the almene sector. Amounts, eligibility and whether sensors qualify were not retrieved — [LBF: puljer til bæredygtighed og digitalisering](https://lbf.dk/nyheder/artikler/nu-er-der-aabnet-for-puljerne-til-baeredygtighed-og-digitalisering) (undated).
- [Fact] Altinget reported that pool billions set aside for almene boliger are not being used — [Altinget: Pulje-milliarder afsat til almene boliger bliver ikke brugt](https://www.altinget.dk/by/artikel/pulje-milliader-afsat-til-almene-boliger-bliver-ikke-brugt) (undated; content not retrieved).
- Sensor providers and prices (Airthings for Business, Kamstrup, Brunata, Techem, ista, Develco/frient, Danish IoT firms): **not found**. The Airthings pricing page was blocked, and no search budget remained.
- Tenders (udbud.dk/TED) for humidity sensors or mould surveys by boligorganisationer: **not found**.
- Prices of mould surveys (skimmelundersøgelse) and remediation: **not found**.

**Evidence-strength label: Weak** for paid demand for monitoring, based on this session's evidence. Tenant rights and municipal guidance establish that the problem exists, not that anyone pays for monitoring.

### Inferences
- **User / buyer / budget holder** [Hyp]: The users are tenants and operations staff (driftspersonale). The buyer in almene housing is the boligorganisation administration, with the afdelingsbestyrelse or organisation board approving. The budget sits in the department's operating budget or planned-maintenance funds, possibly with Landsbyggefonden co-funding (the digitalisation pool above, unverified). The buyer for private landlords is the owner or administrator.
- **Trigger** [Hyp]: A tenant complaint or rent-reduction claim, a municipal mould case, media coverage, or a renovation project. The value to the landlord is documentation of cause (tenant behaviour vs building defect), which matters because liability depends on the cause.
- **Why incumbents might copy** [Hyp]: Metering companies (Brunata, Techem, ista, Kamstrup) likely already have devices and service contracts in large numbers of Danish apartments for heat and water metering. That would give them a cheap bundling path, making differentiation hard for a newcomer. Unverified in this session.
- **Founder fit** [Hyp]: Board work and software product ownership are relevant to selling to boards and building a dashboard. But there is no building-physics, IoT hardware or tender experience, and the long public-procurement sales cycles conflict with limited weekly time. Fit is low to moderate.
- **Capital** [Est, low confidence]: Test (pilot with 20–50 off-the-shelf sensors in one department plus a simple dashboard or reporting service): roughly 30,000–100,000 kr, since sensor unit prices were not found. Launch as a hardware-plus-SaaS product: roughly 0.5–2 m kr. A lower-capital variant is a service that installs third-party sensors and writes cause-documentation reports for landlords.
- **Biggest red flags**: No observed spend in this session. Buyer is tender-driven. Installed-base incumbents. Tenant privacy and data concerns about in-home monitoring [Hyp]. Low demand evidence for paying for prevention rather than for remediation.

### Gaps
- Scale of mould in Danish housing (BUILD/SBi surveys, Landsbyggefonden condition data, Lejernes LO case counts, Huslejenævn/Boligret mould cases): not retrieved; search budget exhausted.
- Whether any 2023–2025 bill or political agreement specifically targeted skimmel in almene boliger. The brief says there was political attention; this session could not confirm or deny it.
- Sensor vendors' Danish prices and business models (Airthings for Business per-device hardware and subscription; Kamstrup, Brunata, Techem, ista humidity or indoor-climate add-ons; Develco/frient; Danish IoT start-ups): not found.

- Prices for mould surveys (typically by building consultants) and remediation per case: not found.
- Number of almene boliger and boligorganisationer: not retrieved in this session.

## Pair 3 – Children's swimming-lesson capacity (private swim school)

### Takeaway
There are visible waiting lists: nearly 300 children in H2Odense, and waits of up to about 12 months at some providers. Private operators already sell lessons at premium prices: 4,000–4,900 kr per 10 × 30-minute lessons, against about 1,800–2,300 kr per season in clubs. The Danish swimming union has itself flagged that private teaching is taking pool space while club waiting lists grow. The binding constraint is access to pool hours, and the founder's PADI diving-instructor qualification has not been shown to be accepted for teaching children to swim. Both points must be verified before this pair can rank highly.

### Inferences
- **User / buyer / budget holder** [Hyp]: The user is a child aged about 3–12. The buyer and budget holder are the parents (household budget). Triggers are school start, summer holidays, a long club waiting list, or a child falling behind. It is seasonal and repeats, with typically several courses per child until they can swim.
- **Alternatives** [Fact/Hyp]: Club teams at 1,800–2,300 kr/season (waiting lists), municipal pool-run courses (Virum, Frederiksberg), school swimming, private premium lessons at 4,000–4,900 kr/10 lessons, or doing nothing and waiting.
- **Why switch** [Hyp]: Immediate start instead of up to 12 months' wait, small groups or one-to-one, intensive holiday courses, and a possible niche: adapted swimming for children with special needs (ADHD, autism, anxiety). That niche directly uses the founder's specialist-education and day-treatment background and could plausibly be sold to municipalities or schools as well as parents.
- **Why incumbents might not copy** [Hyp]: Clubs are volunteer-run and priced low. They struggle to staff specialist or small-group formats, and they are bound to association structures.
- **Founder fit** [Hyp]: Comfort in water and teaching experience help, and work with children with special needs is a real asset. Lessons in afternoons, weekends or holidays could fit limited weekly time. But a PADI recreational scuba-instructor rating is a diving qualification. Whether Danish pools or municipalities accept it for teaching children to swim is unverified. Pool operators commonly require lifeguard or rescue tests for anyone teaching in their water, and child-related work in Denmark normally requires a børneattest. Both are background knowledge, not verified in this session.
- **Capital** [Est]: Test (one 8–10 week course for 2–3 small groups: pool hire at an unknown rate, insurance, rescue course, simple booking page): roughly 10,000–40,000 kr. Launch: roughly 50,000–150,000 kr. Capital exposure is low if pool time can be rented.
- **Biggest red flags**: (1) Pool-time scarcity. Municipalities may prioritise associations over commercial operators, and the federation's headline signals political pushback against private use of pool time. (2) Growth is capped by pool hours. (3) Qualification risk for the founder. (4) Seasonality.
