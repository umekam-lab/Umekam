## Pair 1 — Staff personal alarms / safety systems for Danish social and educational institutions

### Takeaway
This is a real, paid-for and regulated problem. Arbejdstilsynet (the Danish Working Environment Authority) guidance and enforcement orders push institutions towards internal alarm systems that give fast help. Violence rates in døgninstitutioner (residential care) and socialpædagogik (social pedagogy) are high, and many suppliers publish prices of about 149–249 DKK per month per alarm, plus Ascom on a public framework agreement (SKI). The market is crowded. The opening for a newcomer is probably not the alarm itself but reliability that can be shown and checked, plus linking the alarm event to incident documentation and follow-up. That link is a hypothesis: I found no source confirming it as a stated pain point.

### Cited Findings

**Problem, users, buyers**
- [Fact] Arbejdstilsynet's guidance on violence (At-vejledning D.4.3 "Vold") says that, depending on the industry, special measures may be needed: "alarm- og kaldesystemer" (alarm and call systems), TV surveillance, personal protective equipment, workplace layout, escape routes and physical barriers, work organisation, attention to lone working, and behaviour guidelines. It also says the employer must plan and arrange the work so the risk of violence is minimised — [AT D.4.3 Vold](https://regler.at.dk/d-4-3) (undated; also at [at.dk/D-4-3](https://at.dk/D-4-3)).
- [Fact] In a published enforcement order (påbud) titled "Forebyggelse af vold og trusler ved at sikre hurtig og effektiv hjælp", Arbejdstilsynet assessed that police cannot arrive quickly enough to help staff, and that staff cannot get fast help in dangerous situations without an internal alarm system. The order also required enough training and instruction — [Retsinformation, AT-afgørelse 173534](https://www.retsinformation.dk/api/pdf/173534) (date not captured).
- [Fact] Per the search extract, Arbejdstilsynet notes that alarms and GPS "cannot stand alone" as protection, and that staff must not work alone when the risk of violence is increased — [search extract of AT guidance / Folketidende](https://www.folketidende.dk/nyheder/endnu-et-strakspabud-til-bosted-voldsrisiko-kraever-aendringer/5177137) (undated). There is a separate AT decision on lone working in secure institutions: [Retsinformation 152501](https://www.retsinformation.dk/api/pdf/152501) (date not captured).
- [Fact] A local newspaper reports that a bosted (residential facility) received "another strakspåbud" (an immediate enforcement order) because of violence risk — [Folketidende](https://www.folketidende.dk/nyheder/endnu-et-strakspabud-til-bosted-voldsrisiko-kraever-aendringer/5177137) (undated).
- [Fact] An Arbejdstilsynet inspiration case describes the workplace safety representative testing the alarms every month — [AT inspiration case](https://at.dk/arbejdsmiljoe/psykisk-arbejdsmiljoe/vold-og-trusler/forebyggelse-af-vold/inspiration/vi-taler-om-utryghed-derfor-er-vi-trygge/) (undated).
- [Fact] Violence at work is reported as a work accident. A report filed through EASY is routed automatically to Arbejdstilsynet and to the employer's insurer or AES (Arbejdsmarkedets Erhvervssikring, the Danish work-injury authority) — [godtarbejdsmiljo.dk](https://www.godtarbejdsmiljo.dk/trivsel/vold-og-trusler-paa-arbejdspladsen/krisehjaelp-og-opfoelgning/anmeldelse-af-en-voldsepisode) (undated).
- [Fact] Use of force (magtanvendelse) in social services must be registered and reported on set forms. Monthly reports are due by the 5th of the following month, and municipalities or regions review them and prepare annual reports — [Social- og Boligstyrelsen, registrering og indberetning](https://www.sbst.dk/boern/anbringelse/voksenansvar-og-forebyggelse-af-magtanvendelse/registrering-og-indberetning) (undated); [SBST vejledning, file dated 26.08.24](https://www.sbst.dk/Media/638604414040429351/2_Vejledning%20magtanvendelse%2026.08.24%20-%20til%20offentligg%C3%B8relse%20hjemmeside.pdf).
- [Fact] Alarm or tracking devices aimed at *residents* (for example door alarms) need a formal municipal decision under the Serviceloven (Social Services Act), and the Ombudsman found "significant errors" in one municipality's use of door alarms in a botilbud (residential care home) — [Ombudsmanden](https://www.ombudsmanden.dk/find/nyheder/alle/doeralarmer_i_botilbud/) (undated). This matters for product design: a staff alarm must not drift into monitoring residents.

**Size of the problem / cost of leaving it unresolved**
- [Fact] Police received 2,434 reports of violence and threats against public employees in H1 2025, and 4,500–5,000 per year since 2022 — [Justitsministeriet press release](https://www.justitsministeriet.dk/pressemeddelelse/nye-tal-taet-paa-2-500-anmeldelser-om-vold-og-trusler-mod-offentligt-ansatte-i-foerste-halvaar-af-2025/) (2025; exact date not captured).
- [Fact] Among people in public service, those doing social and health work had the largest numerical increase in violence, and staff doing special-pedagogical work also increased — [Danmarks Statistik NYT](https://www.dst.dk/da/Statistik/udgivelser/NytHtml?cid=21562) (date not captured).
- [Fact] In the January 2023 Socialpædagogerne (SL) member survey, 27% had experienced physical violence and 31% psychological violence in the past 12 months. In 2014 the figures were 36% violence and 33% threats — [SL via Ritzau: "Hver fjerde socialpædagog bliver udsat for vold"](https://via.ritzau.dk/pressemeddelelse/ny-undersogelse-hver-fjerde-socialpaedagog-bliver-udsat-for-vold-pa-arbejdet?publisherId=13561380&releaseId=13678600) (2023); [SL vold og trusler](https://sl.dk/arbejdsliv-og-vilkaar/arbejdsmiljoe/vold-og-trusler/) (undated).

- [Fact] Reported work accidents classed as "akut psykisk overbelastning" (acute psychological overload) rose 46% from 2019 to 2023 — [Folketinget BEU bilag 132, FAKTA, Feb 2025](https://www.ft.dk/samling/20241/almdel/beu/bilag/132/2980132.pdf).
- [Fact] Copenhagen Municipality reports that a rising number of teachers and pedagogues are exposed to violence at work — [kk.dk](https://www.kk.dk/nyheder/et-stigende-antal-laerere-og-paedagoger-udsaettes-for-vold-paa-arbejdspladsen) (undated; figures not captured).
- [Fact] Ascom (a supplier, so a biased source) argues that municipal bosteder face high sick leave, long-term sick leave and staff leaving the labour market because of an unsafe work environment — [Ascom blog](https://www.ascom.com/da/nyheder/blog/dk-blogs/betaler-en-hoj-pris-for-det-utrygge-arbejdsmiljo/) (undated; no DKK figures in the extract).

**Regulation / certification**

- [Hypothesis / background knowledge, not verified this session] The executive order on the psychosocial work environment (BEK 1406/2020) requires the workplace risk assessment (APV) to cover work-related violence. ### Inferences
- **Precise problem [Hypothesis]:** Staff in bosteder, specialskoler, dagbehandling, psychiatry and jobcentre need help to arrive within seconds and to know the alarm will work. Managers need to show Arbejdstilsynet they have fast help, training and follow-up, and to document incidents (the AT/EASY work-accident report, and for residents the magtanvendelse forms).
- **User:** front-line staff. **Buyer / budget holder [Hypothesis]:** the institution leader, with the arbejdsmiljøorganisation (the workplace safety committee). In municipalities, purchasing is often central, through SKI or municipal IT/procurement. Private and independent operators (private bosteder, friskoler/specialskoler, dagbehandlingstilbud) decide locally and faster.
- **Purchase triggers [Hypothesis, supported by cited påbud and news]:** an Arbejdstilsynet påbud or strakspåbud, a serious incident, a new building or unit, the renewal date of an existing contract, or a union/safety-representative push. Frequency: a 3–6 year system lifecycle, plus a monthly subscription.
- **Alternatives:** doing nothing (shouting, mobile phones), fixed panic buttons, DECT or legacy nurse-call based personal alarms, 24/7 alarm-centre subscriptions (CEKURA, Alarmselskabet), app plus button (Linucare), enterprise systems (Ascom).
- **Why switch / newcomer improvement [Hypothesis]:** (1) reliability that can be seen and checked: device heartbeat, automated daily self-tests with a log that can be shown to Arbejdstilsynet, and signalling that does not depend on the IT supplier (the Aalborg failure is a strong sales story); (2) linking alarm event → structured incident report → follow-up and APV analytics, cutting the double registration between the EASY accident report, internal violence registration and magtanvendelse forms. This is where the founder's documentation experience fits. (3) Pricing and setup made for small private operators (5–40 staff), who are poorly served by enterprise sales.
- **Why incumbents may not copy [Hypothesis]:** Ascom sells a large platform through frameworks and targets hospitals. Alarm-centre firms sell a monthly guard service and are not documentation- or pedagogy-oriented. However, any of them could add an incident form, so the moat is thin unless it comes from sector-specific workflow and integrations (e.g., with resident-record systems).
- **Founder fit:** High. The founder has deep domain credibility with leaders, safety representatives and boards in specialskoler and dagbehandling. The documentation-tool experience carries over directly to the incident-follow-up layer, and direct sales suits a fragmented market of private institutions. The limit is limited weekly time against a safety-critical, round-the-clock product: this needs a partner for alarm handling (an existing alarm centre) and hardware (white-label).
- **Capital [Estimate]:** Test: DKK 25–75k (white-label buttons or app from an existing supplier, 3–5 pilot sites, a GDPR/DPIA review (data-protection impact assessment), a simple incident-report module built on the existing tool). Launch as a software/service layer with a partner alarm centre: DKK 0.5–2m. Own hardware development: DKK 3–10m+ (not recommended).
- **Biggest red flags:** product liability and reputation if an alarm fails (see Aalborg). Municipal buyers use frameworks (SKI) or tenders, with long cycles. Price competition at 149–249 DKK/month. Staff location tracking raises GDPR and works-council sensitivity, and anything touching residents triggers Serviceloven rules. The founder's limited time conflicts with 24/7 support duties.
- **Preliminary ratings (1–5):** demand evidence **4**; ability to reach buyers **4**; founder fit **5**; contribution margin **3**; differentiation **3**; execution feasibility **2**; capital exposure **3**; growth potential **3**. Confidence: **medium**.

## Pair 2 — Lithium-ion e-bike / e-scooter / mobility-scooter battery fire risk in Danish housing, schools and workplaces

### Takeaway
Battery fires are rising steadily (143 fires from smaller lithium-ion products in 2024, about 3× the 2020 level, with small vehicles the largest category), and many fire-safe charging cabinets are on sale in Denmark with public prices (about DKK 9k–73k). But the absolute number of fires is low, the rules are advice only (no charging-cabinet mandate found), and the products are commodities sold by industrial safety distributors. Demand evidence is medium, and the founder has little specific advantage here.

### Cited Findings

**Problem size**
- [Fact] Beredskabsstyrelsen (BRS, the Danish Emergency Management Agency) fact sheet 2025: 143 fires started in smaller lithium-ion products in 2024 (+9% vs 2023, about 3× the 55 in 2020). "Smaller means of transport" (e-bikes, scooters, hoverboards, e-scooters) is the largest category. Fires in waste with a suspected lithium-ion cause went from 12 (2023) to 24 (2024). 647,344 imported e-bikes in 2024 (+59,723 vs 2023; +353,539 since 2020; the extract does not make clear whether this is a stock or a cumulative-import figure) — [BRS Faktaark 2025](https://www.brs.dk/globalassets/brs---beredskabsstyrelsen/dokumenter/faktaark/--faktaark-om-brand-i-mindre-litiumionprodukter2024_v8-.pdf) (2025); [TV 2, 2025-09-11](https://nyheder.tv2.dk/samfund/2025-09-11-antallet-af-batteribrande-eksploderet).
- [Fact] About one-third of these fires occur during charging. The fire services recommend being present during charging, or charging where there is little flammable material — [TV 2, 2025-09-11](https://nyheder.tv2.dk/samfund/2025-09-11-antallet-af-batteribrande-eksploderet).
- [Fact] Earlier series: 30 fires in 2021, 73 in 2022, 58 in H1 2023. 40% of small-vehicle fires in 2018–2022 occurred during charging. Battery fires up 330% since 2018 (the extract combined figures from several pages) — [Tænk](https://taenk.dk/forbrugerliv/elektronik-og-digitale-tjenester/hvor-sikre-er-de-genopladelige-batterier); [F&P "Pas på brandfare"](https://fogp.dk/nyheder/pas-paa-brandfare-naar-elcyklen-lader-op/); [BRS 2022 analysis](https://www.brs.dk/da/nyheder/2022/fokusanalyse-elcykler/) (dates: 2022–2023).
- [Fact] The BRS 2022 focus analysis was titled "Elcykler og elløbehjul brænder sjældent" (e-bikes and e-scooters rarely burn) — [BRS 2022](https://www.brs.dk/da/nyheder/2022/fokusanalyse-elcykler/). This is an important counter-signal on how severe the problem is.
- [Fact] Forsikring & Pension (F&P, the insurance industry association) counted 84 fires caused by lithium-ion batteries in 2018–2021, some from e-bike batteries — [F&P](https://fogp.dk/nyheder/pas-paa-brandfare-naar-elcyklen-lader-op/) (undated).
- [Fact] Incidents: April 2024, an e-bike charging on a balcony in Copenhagen Nordvest caused a large fire with smoke in the building and evacuation. An e-bike battery fire in Aalborg also led to evacuation — [TV 2, 2024-04-19](https://nyheder.tv2.dk/samfund/2024-04-19-batteri-skyld-i-kaempebrand-den-kan-man-ikke-bare-lige-slukke-siger-ekspert); [TV2 Nord](https://www.tv2nord.dk/aalborg/batteri-til-el-cykel-broed-i-brand-beboere-blev-evakueret) (undated).
- [Fact] BRS and Sikkerhedsstyrelsen (the Danish Safety Technology Authority) are working together to identify the biggest challenges — [Sikkerhedsstyrelsen](https://www.sik.dk/nyheder/fortsat-stigning-antal-brande-mindre-litiumionbatterier) (2024). Teknologisk Institut wrote a report for BRS on new building trends — [BRS/TI, 19.11.2025](https://www.brs.dk/globalassets/brs---beredskabsstyrelsen/dokumenter/faktaark/-nye-bygningstendenser-og-deres-betydning-for-det-danske-redningsberedskab---beredskabsstyrelsen-19.11.2025-.pdf) (content not captured).
- [Fact] If Forsikring put out a press release on a "big increase" in battery fires in Danish homes with 6 tips (figures not captured) — [If via Ritzau](https://via.ritzau.dk/pressemeddelelse/14576246/stor-stigning-i-danske-hjem-her-er-6-gode-rad-til-at-undga-farlige-batteribrande?publisherId=11341474&lang=da) (undated).

**Rules and recommendations**
- [Fact] Sikkerhedsstyrelsen: in older buildings' basements, residents often chain extension leads, power strips and "snydestik" (cheater plugs) to charge e-bikes, which can be illegal and a real fire hazard. It recommends a fixed installation with dedicated sockets on their own circuit. Arc-fault detection devices (AFDD) are *only a recommendation* in Denmark. The separate RCD/circuit requirement does not apply to e-bike charging, where the motor is only an auxiliary motor — [Sikkerhedsstyrelsen, opladning af elcykler](https://www.sik.dk/erhverv/elinstallationer-og-elanlaeg/vejledninger/elinstallationer/boliger/opladning-elcykler) (undated). Implication: any fixed installation in a bike room or basement needs an authorised electrician.
- [Fact] Stairwells and hallways are escape routes and must not be used for storage — [Andelsportal FAQ](https://www.andelsportal.dk/nyheder/faq-ting-opgang/) (undated); [Nordjyllands Beredskab leaflet](https://www.nordjyllandsberedskab.dk/sites/default/files/Til%20dig%20der%20bor%20i%20en%20etageboligejendom.pdf) (undated). Fire-service charging advice: [Nordsjællands Brandvæsen](https://nsbv.dk/videnscenter/opladning/); [Danske Beredskaber](https://danskeberedskaber.dk/batterier-og-opladning/) (undated).
- [Fact] Højesteret (the Supreme Court) ruled that tenants in almene boliger (non-profit social housing) have no right under the installation-right rules to install an EV charger. So decisions on charging infrastructure sit with the housing organisation rather than individual tenants — [BygTek](https://bygtek.dk/artikel/el-lys/forbudt-at-installere-ladeboks-i-almen-bolig) (undated).
- **Not found:** any Danish legal requirement for fire-rated charging cabinets in housing, schools or workplaces, or specific insurer demands (e.g., premium conditions) for housing associations.

**Insurer positions**
- [Fact] Standard e-bikes (≤25 km/h) are covered by indbo (contents) and liability insurance; faster bikes (speed pedelecs) need their own insurance — [If](https://www.if.dk/privat/forsikringer/bolig/indboforsikring/elcykel); [Cykelexperten](https://cykelexperten.dk/viden/elcykel/skal-man-have-forsikring-paa-sin-elcykel/) (undated).
- [Fact] F&P publishes charging-safety advice (warning, not mandate) — [F&P](https://fogp.dk/nyheder/pas-paa-brandfare-naar-elcyklen-lader-op/).
- [Fact] Secondary sources say a housing association's insurance should be updated when charging stations are put in common areas (this was about EV chargers) — [search extract, various](https://nationenblog.dk/forsikring-af-ladestander-er-der-krav-danmark/) (undated; weak source).

**Evidence of spending — evidence strength: MEDIUM** (several competitors with public prices; claims of installations at residential properties; no tenders or sales volumes found)
- [Fact] Newtek (exclusive Danish distributor of ECO Charge since early 2023) says ECO Charge cabinets (3–12 compartments, ventilation and heating for outdoor use) are installed at train stations, large workplaces, educational institutions and residential properties — [Newtek](https://www.newtek.dk/elcykler-ladeskabe); [Byggematerialer.dk](https://www.byggematerialer.dk/eco-charge-ladeskabe-fra-newtek-325296/nyhed.html) (undated). ## Pair 3 — Water-leak detection and automatic shut-off for Danish homes and housing associations

### Takeaway
Water damage is one of the biggest and best-documented property-claim categories, and spending on prevention is clearly visible. But the market is already structured and subsidised by insurers and utilities: Topdanmark offers LeakBot to 200,000 customers at 60 DKK/month, If and Alka give 10–25% discounts on approved systems, and water utilities send free SMS leak alarms from remote-read meters. Demand is real, but the chance for a newcomer to differentiate or capture margin is poor, and founder fit is weak.

### Cited Findings

**Size of the problem**
- [Fact] Nearly 94,000 building water-damage claims in 2018 with DKK 2.1bn paid out, according to F&P (secondary citation) — [Brunata](https://brunata.dk/undgaa-vandskader-med-en-vandalarm/) / [WiceIoT](https://www.wiceiot.dk/undgaa-vandskade/) (undated; the extract did not show which of the two carried the figure).
- [Fact] 70,000 homeowners reported water damage from pipe leaks in 2022, up from 46,000 in 2010. - [Fact, secondary] Typical water-damage payout DKK 25,000–150,000; large hidden-pipe leaks lasting weeks DKK 300,000–500,000; deductibles DKK 5,000–10,000; "over 60,000 notifications per year" (attributed to F&P) — [Fælles Forsikring](https://www.fforsikring.dk/husforsikring/vandskade) (undated; weak — insurer marketing page repeating F&P).
