## 2. The initial offer

- **What we sell:** a practitioner-led **walkthrough and room plan** (about 2 hours on site, plan within 5 working days), then **fixed-price robust room packages**. Each package covers supply, delivery, installation, the maker's test documentation, and a 3-month follow-up.
- **To whom:** leaders and owners of private and self-governing residential units, specialist schools and day-treatment units serving outward-reacting children, young people or adults, within about 90 minutes' drive.
- **Outcome for the buyer:** rooms that stay furnished and homely instead of being stripped. Fewer broken items and fewer objects that can be thrown. A documented change to the physical setting for the violence risk assessment, the board, and inspections by Arbejdstilsynet or the social inspectorate. One person who understands the work, instead of a catalogue.
- **Price hypothesis** (ex VAT; revise once price lists arrive):
  - walkthrough and room plan: **DKK 2,500**, credited in full against an order within 60 days
  - robust bedroom package: **DKK 18,000–40,000**
  - calm common room: **DKK 35,000–90,000**
  - single items: at list price
  - pilot terms: 30% deposit, balance on installation, 5% pilot discount in return for before-and-after documentation (never showing people) and a reference if the customer is satisfied
- **What we do not promise:** that we prevent violence or self-harm. Anti-ligature claims are passed on only as the maker has documented them.

## 5. Customer interview guide

**Rules:** ask about the past, not the future. No pitching until the last two minutes. Record numbers. Never record information about residents or pupils.

**Five core questions:**
1. "Think of the last time furniture or fixtures here were broken, thrown or had to be removed for safety. What happened, and what did you do in the days after?"
2. "Over the last 12 months, what did you replace, fix or take out of rooms because of damage or safety? Roughly what did it cost, whose budget paid for it, and who approved it?"
3. "How do you buy furniture today: which supplier or agreement, who decides, and how long does it take from need to installed item?"
4. "What have you tried to make rooms both safe and homely, including custom joinery, fixing things down or emptying rooms? What worked, and what didn't?"
5. "What is your next planned change: new residents or pupils, a new unit, a renovation, an inspection? Is money set aside for it?"

**Two probe questions for the alternative (B):**
- "What alarm do staff carry today, what does it cost a month, and when does the contract end?"
- "When did an alarm last fail or come too late, and what happens on paper after a violent incident, meaning which forms and who fills them in?"

**Close:** "Would a 2-hour walkthrough with a fixed-price room plan be worth DKK 2,500 to you, credited against an order? Could we book a date?" Record yes, no or maybe, and the reason.

## 7. Smallest practical demonstration

The central assumption is that a leader will pay for a practitioner's plan and a robust package. The smallest test needs no own product:

1. **A walkthrough checklist** (A4) covering:
   - where and when incidents happen
   - items thrown, broken or removed in the last 12 months
   - fixing options
   - sightlines and escape routes
   - ligature points (only where the resident or pupil group makes it relevant)
   - cleanability
   - noise and light
   - homely elements
   - storage of sharp objects
   - alarm placement (the B probe)
   - budget and timing
2. **Three package sheets** (bedroom, common room, calm corner) with product photos and specifications from the chosen maker's catalogue, used with permission. Until price lists arrive, use clearly labelled price ranges.
3. **One physical sample if a maker will lend or sell one** (for example a weighted chair), plus fabric and material swatches.
4. **A one-page before-and-after visual** of an empty example room. Never photograph a customer's residents or pupils.

This tests willingness to pay (a paid walkthrough and letters of intent with prices) and delivery (supplier terms converted into a fixed quote) without stock, tooling or company registration.

- **Continue** needs all of M2, M3 and M4 to be green.
